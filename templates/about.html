How SafeZone works

Three simple steps to find a safe place near you.

★

1. Sign in

Create an account or sign in. Your role determines what you can do — user or administrator.

★

2. Find shelters

Share your location or enter it manually. Browse verified shelters on the map or in a list, filtered by status and facilities.

★

3. Get a route

Request a road route to a shelter. It’s checked against recorded hazard zones so you’re warned of affected areas.

Roles & permissions

Registered user
  • ✓ View dashboard, alerts and emergency contacts
  • ✓ Share or enter a location
  • ✓ Search, filter and view shelter details
  • ✓ Request routes with hazard checks
  • ✗ Cannot create shelters or publish alerts
Administrator
  • ✓ Create, edit, verify and deactivate shelters
  • ✓ Draw and manage hazard polygons
  • ✓ Draft, publish and expire alerts
  • ✓ Manage emergency contacts and review audit log
  • ✗ Cannot bypass authentication
